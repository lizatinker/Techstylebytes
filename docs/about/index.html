Hi, I’m Liza.

I’m a Microsoft MVP with more than 25 years in SharePoint and Microsoft 365. I live in Sydney, and I refuse to believe tech has to be grey.

I paint big, bold canvases, design my own t-shirts, and come from a visual merchandising background. So I notice the details: the plate, the light, the lining of a jacket, the sticker on a laptop.

Tech Style Bytes is my notebook of Sydney cafes, bars, art, streets, fashion and the tech I actually use. It’s also where I share how I work from home and run my own business with AI, smart tools and good systems. All honest reviews, all my own photos.
