If you love keyboards, Keydom in Chippendale is your happy place.

It's a store full of keyboards, mice and headphones, made for keyboard enthusiasts. I've been in a few times now, just wandering around tapping keyboards. It's so much fun.

> So many keyboards to choose from, all in one place. I don't think there's another store like it in Sydney.

![A lit wall of mechanical keyboards under the Keydom sign](../../images/keydom-lit-keyboard-display.jpg)

## What you'll find

- **Mechanical keyboards:** walls of them, and you can try them all out.
- **Custom builds:** you can have your keyboard custom made.
- **Keycaps:** you can have keycaps done too.
- **Mice and headphones:** everything else for your desk setup.

## Why I love it

Keyboard collecting is a bit of a craze online. People collect them, and I'm one of them. I'm just starting my collection.

Here you can actually try them before you choose. The store has been open for about a year, and if you haven't been yet, it's worth the trip.

## Who it's for

- **Keyboard collectors:** this is your place.
- **Anyone building a desk setup:** keyboards, mice and headphones in one place.
- **The curious:** head to Chippendale and have a tap.

**My verdict:** A unique store for tech lovers. If you're into keyboards, or just curious, make sure you check it out.
