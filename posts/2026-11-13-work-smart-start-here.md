I've spent 35 years in workplaces. Most of that time I've been the person who maps the process, finds the gaps and then builds something better in Microsoft 365.

So when I started running my own business from home, I did what I always do. I mapped it.

## Why I'm sharing this

Tech Style Bytes is where I test tools and live the style. Until now, that's meant cafés, keyboards, laneways and outfits.

But the tools I use most are the ones that run my business. They write, schedule, organise, remind and publish. Some of them are AI. Some of them are just good, boring systems that never let me down.

This new section, **Work Smart**, is my notebook of all of it.

## What I'm building

I run **Simply SharePoint**, my SharePoint and Microsoft 365 business: guides, toolkits, consulting, a podcast and a newsletter.

I've now set AI up like a small team. There's an executive assistant for email, a chief of staff for the whole business, and a marketing agent for Instagram, reels and content. The goal is to run the business like a machine, so I can spend my energy on the parts I love.

This blog is part of the experiment too. I send photos and a voice note, and it becomes a post.
